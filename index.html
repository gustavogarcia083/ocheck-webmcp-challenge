    Built at 43 years old

    Human impact · Colombia → the world

    What if expertise could become software—without first becoming a software engineer?

    OCHECK was founded by Gustavo García Figueroa, a Colombian lawyer with intermediate English and no traditional software background. Using ChatGPT and Codex as working tools, and Vercel as deployment infrastructure, he moved from a persistent real-world problem to product strategy, governance, UX, and a functioning WebMCP experience with global ambition.

    “OpenAI did not replace my judgment or responsibility. It gave me the leverage to turn them into a product.”

    This is not only a founder story. It is evidence of a much larger opportunity: domain experts everywhere can turn lived experience into useful software when technology learns to work with human intent instead of demanding that every human first learn to think like a machine.
