    Human impact · Colombia → the world

    What if one person’s imagination could reach the execution power of a technical team?

    OCHECK was founded by Gustavo García Figueroa, a Colombian lawyer with intermediate English and no traditional software background. Using ChatGPT and Codex as working tools, and Vercel as deployment infrastructure, he moved from a persistent real-world problem to product strategy, governance, customer experience, tests, and a functioning WebMCP product with global ambition.

    “The human mind has always had limits. Until now.”

    Gustavo brought the idea, inventiveness, product judgment, and accountability. ChatGPT and Codex provided execution leverage—turning language into flows, code, tests, and iteration. Technology did not replace the human mind; it expanded what one determined person could build with it.
